Project 01

Dynamic Wireless Charging Station for Electric Two-Wheeler

Designed and prototyped an inductive power transfer system enabling on-the-go charging for EV two-wheelers, with optimized coil alignment and efficient power electronics.

75%

Power Transfer Efficiency

100mm

How It Works

  • Roadside coils create alternating magnetic fields.
  • Vehicle coils receive energy in real time.
  • Magnetic resonance keeps charging efficient despite misalignment.
  • High-frequency inverters optimize the power transfer.

Benefits & Challenges

  • Continuous charging for extended range.
  • Reduced battery size and cost.
  • Challenge: high infrastructure cost.
  • Challenge: standardization and safety.
